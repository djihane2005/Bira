Our Identity

Innovation at the service of the Algerian building

Birabrick is an Algerian company focused on producing sustainable construction materials by transforming local and recycled resources into high-performance eco-bricks. It develops two main types of bricks: fired dredged mud bricks and geopolymer bricks, combining environmental responsibility with engineering efficiency.

These bricks are designed to be lightweight, reducing structural loads and facilitating construction, while offering strong durability and excellent thermal insulation to improve energy efficiency and indoor comfort. Through this approach, Birabrick contributes to a more sustainable and low-carbon future for the construction sector in Algeria.
